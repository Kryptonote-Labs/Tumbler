# Third-party components

Compressed EOT decoding uses [mtx-decompressor](https://github.com/ChristopherVR/mtx-decompressor), distributed under MPL-2.0. Its source and license are available in the linked repository and installed package. Tumbler calls the unmodified package.

The Svelte metafile renderer uses [rtf.js](https://github.com/tbluemel/rtf.js), distributed under MIT. Its EMF and WMF renderers convert supported Windows metafile records into SVG in the browser.
