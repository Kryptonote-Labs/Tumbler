# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Discuss ideas and evidence without
attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption,
and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior harms contributors or the project. Serious or sensitive concerns
should be reported privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security contact rather than argued in public.

This standard applies in repository discussions and in other spaces where
someone is representing the Tumbler project.
